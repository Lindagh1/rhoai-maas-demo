# Acme Corp — New Hire Onboarding Guide

**Document ID:** HR-POL-005
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Last reviewed:** April 2026

## Week 1

- Day 1: Laptop pickup at the IT desk, badge activation, and a welcome
  session with your manager.
- Day 2–3: Mandatory compliance training — Code of Conduct, IT Security
  Baseline Policy, and workplace safety.
- Day 4–5: Meet-and-greet rounds with your immediate team and cross-team
  stakeholders identified by your manager.

## First 30 days

New hires are expected to work on-site every day during the first 30 days,
regardless of the standard Monday/Friday remote work allowance, so that
managers and teammates can build working relationships in person. After the
30-day mark and successful completion of onboarding checklists, new hires
gain access to the standard remote work policy, including the Monday and
Friday remote days.

## Systems access

Access to Jira, Confluence, the internal model catalog, and the expense
system is provisioned automatically within 24 hours of your start date.
Access to production systems requires a manager-approved ticket and
completion of the IT Security Baseline training.

## Buddy program

Every new hire is paired with a "buddy" from a different team for the first
90 days to help answer day-to-day questions that don't require going
through a manager or HR.

## Probation period

The standard probation period is 90 days. Benefits enrollment (medical,
dental, vision, 401(k)) is available from day one, independent of probation
status.
