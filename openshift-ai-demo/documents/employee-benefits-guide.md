# Acme Corp — Employee Benefits Guide

**Document ID:** HR-POL-021
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Last reviewed:** February 2026

## Health and wellness

Acme Corp covers 100% of the premium for the base medical, dental, and
vision plans for the employee and 50% for dependents. Employees can enroll
or make changes during the annual open enrollment window in November, or
within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

## Paid time off

- 22 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.
- 10 paid holidays per year, published each December for the following year.
- Unlimited paid sick leave, self-certified for absences of two days or
  fewer.

## Retirement

Acme Corp matches 401(k)/pension contributions up to 5% of base salary,
vested immediately. Enrollment is available from day one of employment.

## Learning and development

Every employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses,
books, or conference attendance, plus 5 paid "learning days" that can be
used at any time with manager notification (not approval).

## Wellness stipend

A monthly $60 wellness stipend can be used for gym memberships, fitness
apps, or mental health apps of the employee's choice, reimbursed through the
expense system with a receipt.

## Relationship to remote work

Employees who use the standard Monday/Friday remote work allowance remain
fully eligible for all benefits above; benefits eligibility is based on
employment status, not work location.
