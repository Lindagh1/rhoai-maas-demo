# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-014
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Last reviewed:** March 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines when and how Acme Corp employees may work remotely instead
of attending the office in person.

## Eligible days

Employees may work remotely on **Monday and Friday**. All other weekdays
(Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) are designated in-office collaboration days for
every team, unless a manager grants an explicit written exception for medical,
caregiving, or relocation reasons.

## Requesting remote work

1. Employees do not need pre-approval for the standard Monday/Friday remote
   days — they are a default benefit for all full-time staff after the
   90-day probation period.
2. Any additional remote day beyond Monday and Friday requires manager
   approval through the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance.
3. Remote work is not permitted during scheduled on-site events, quarterly
   business reviews, or client visits, even if they fall on a Monday or
   Friday.

## Equipment and expectations

Employees working remotely must remain reachable on chat and video during
core hours (10:00–16:00 local time) and must attend all meetings with camera
enabled unless otherwise agreed with their manager. Acme Corp provides a
one-time home-office stipend of $500 for eligible employees, administered via
the IT Asset Management team.

## Exceptions

Interns and contractors are not covered by this policy and follow the terms
of their individual contracts. Roles requiring physical presence (lab
technicians, on-site security, facilities staff) are excluded from the
Monday/Friday remote allowance.
